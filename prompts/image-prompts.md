# AI Image Prompts

This folder contains prompts used to create visual scenes for Football Special videos.

## Purpose

The prompts are designed to help create:

- Cinematic football visuals
- Consistent characters
- Storytelling scenes
- 9:16 vertical compositions
- Visually connected scenes

## Prompting Approach

Each scene prompt describes:

1. The footballer or character
2. Their age and appearance
3. The environment
4. Clothing
5. The action taking place
6. Camera angle
7. Lighting
8. Cinematic style
9. Vertical 9:16 composition

## Example

> A young footballer walking through a modest neighborhood in Madeira at sunset, carrying a worn football, cinematic realistic photography, emotional atmosphere, warm natural lighting, detailed environment, vertical 9:16 composition.
